# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a wallet project. Do not harass people, do not demand a
seed phrase, and do not use the issue tracker to phish.

## Expected

- Kind, specific, technical discussion.
- Respect for people who write in Polish, English, German, Russian, or Spanish.
- Patience with newcomers who are learning what "non-custodial" means.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, slurs, or sexual attention that is not invited.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Asking a user to reveal a seed, a passphrase, or a pool secret.
- Selling "guaranteed mining returns" under the Hashly name.

## Reports

Open a GitHub issue for conduct that is not a security bug. For a vulnerability,
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The maintainers of Hashly / Infinitas will
look at reports and may hide comments, lock threads, or block accounts.

This text is specific to this repository. It is not the Contributor Covenant,
and it is not a promise of a particular legal process.
