## The software

already operate. It does not hold funds, does not sell hashrate, does not
process card, BLIK, or cryptocurrency payments, and does not promise a return.

Mining estimates use public price and difficulty data and the numbers you
type. They can be wrong. You can lose money. Nothing in the app is financial,
tax, or mining advice.

## Your responsibilities

- You keep the seed and the passphrase. They cannot be reset.
- You follow the law that applies to you, including tax on mining income.
- You use pool keys you are allowed to use.
- You do not point the LAN connector at a miner you do not own.
- You review a mainnet transaction before you broadcast it. Broadcasting is a
  separate, explicit action.
